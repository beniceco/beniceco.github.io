How many watts does each device actually draw while charging?

Chargers are sold by their maximum output, but a device only takes what it can use. This table lists typical peak charging power by device type, so you can tell when a bigger charger helps and when it makes no difference.

Why the peak does not last

Phones charge fastest when the battery is nearly empty. Above roughly 50 to 80 percent they lower the current to protect the cell, so the average over a full charge is well below the headline number. This is why a 45 W charger and a 25 W charger often finish at almost the same time on the same phone.

What "enough" means

A charger rated higher than the device needs is safe: the device negotiates the power it wants over USB Power Delivery and the charger supplies no more. A charger rated lower is also safe, but slower. The only real risk is a counterfeit charger that ignores the rules; buying from the device maker or a known brand avoids that.

Figures are typical ranges from manufacturer specifications and published tests, not measurements of any single model. Check your device's own documentation for its exact maximum.
