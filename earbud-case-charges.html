How many times can an earbud case recharge the buds?

Earbud specifications usually give two playtime figures: one per charge of the buds, and a larger total "with case". The case is a small power bank, and the number of recharges it gives follows from three numbers.

The rule of thumb

Recharges ≈ case capacity × about 0.7 ÷ (capacity of both buds together). The 0.7 accounts for the case's own electronics, the step from the case battery to the buds' charging contacts, and the buds' charging circuit. A case with 500 mAh charging two 50 mAh buds gives about 500 × 0.7 ÷ 100 = 3.5 full recharges.

Checking the "total playtime" claim

Multiply the per-charge playtime by one plus the number of recharges. If buds play 6 hours and the case gives 3.5 recharges, the total is about 6 × 4.5 = 27 hours. A listed total far above this usually assumes noise cancelling off and low volume.

Why the number falls over time

Both the case and the buds use small lithium cells that lose capacity with every charge cycle, so after a year or two both the per-charge playtime and the number of recharges drop. Keeping the case out of heat helps more than anything else.
